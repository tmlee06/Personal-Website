I have been to Vancouver at least 3 times, but I "lost" or did not take photos from my visit.

Every visit for Vancouver was for hockey and nothing else. Vancouver is a very nice and clean city, but no Toronto 🦉. The city probably cleaned up because of the 2011 Stanley Cup Final riots 😂. 

 ![I would be mad too, if this was the Leafs.](https://i.cbc.ca/ais/1.6822262,1682458048000/full/max/0/default.jpg?im=Crop%2Crect%3D%280%2C0%2C1600%2C1062%29%3B)

Aside from hockey, Vancouver is nothing short of fun. If you are not a Canucks fan, which is probably a lot of Vancouverites after Quinn Hughes got traded, then Vancouver is a great place to live. I have very fond memories of watching the 2018 World Junior Championship and skiing Grouse Mountain. Skiing Whistler is up there in my bucket list.
