I was too young and busy playing hockey to really understand and get a feel for Calgary as a city, but the one day I spent downtown, I thought it was a pretty enjoyable. I would not necessarily want to live here (or root for the Flames).

This may sound disrespectful, but I believe Calgary is the definition of a "layover city". I never had a desire to travel back other than visiting and skating on a frozen Banff lake during the winter. You could probably explore all of Calgary in under 24 hours. I see why Tate McRae got out of there. That's my 12-year-old self and Toronto bias talking, but I still feel the same way.
