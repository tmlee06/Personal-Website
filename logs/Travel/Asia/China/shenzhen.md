I was only there for less than 12 hours, but Shenzhen is one of the most impressive cities I have ever visited. Shenzhen is what one would imagine Silicon Valley would look like after hearing it's the "richest large metropolitan area in the world."

The High Speed Rail from Kowloon Station to Shenzhen is about 15 minutes, maybe even less. I messed up and went to Shenzhenbei Station when I probably should have gone to Futian Station.
