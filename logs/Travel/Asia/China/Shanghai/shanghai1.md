A little history, these European style buildings are here because China lost the Opium Wars. The British forced China to sign the **Treaty of Nanjing in 1842** and opened up Shanghai to foreign trade. The British came first, then the Americans and French settled in as well too. China was carved up into "*spheres of influences*", where Western powers and Japan took control of the trade and economy in different regions. The European style buildings on The Bund were a built during the peak of the foreign control era as Shanghai was and is still a powerhouse for Western banks and trading companies in Asia (i.e. *HSBC - Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation*).

The European buildings are really cool, and maybe next time I visit China, I can see their knock-off Paris 🤣.
