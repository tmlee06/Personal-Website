Early morning flights are always brutal and pulling an all-nighter before is probably the worst thing you can do, which is exactly what I did 😂.

The day before the flight I had two finals: Japanese and Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence. On top of that, I had to finish two assignments. Although they were not due explicitly that day, I wanted to ensure I submitted them because I was not going to take a chance with <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Firewall" style="color: #fffefe; font-weight: bold; text-decoration: underline;">The Great Firewall</a>. 

Flight was 9:05 AM, and my travel partner, Cole, and I wanted to be at the airport by 6 AM since it is an international flight, and we were still pretty skeptical about Trip.com since $927 for 4 flights is an insane deal. The problem with getting to the airport at 6 is that the first train from Kyoto heading that direction is at 5:00 AM and the commute is about 2 hours. Cole lives directly above the station, so catching the train is very convenient for him, but not me. My dorm is quite far, and the bus does not run before 5 AM, so I had to leave my dorm at 3:45 AM and walk to the station for our 4:50 AM meetup time.

By the time I got to the station, I was drenched in sweat. Surprisingly, the walk only took me 40 minutes and I had about 15 minutes to spare before Cole arrived. I must say Kyoto during the quiet hours of 4-6 AM is the most beautiful and peaceful city in the world. Walking along the Kamo Gawa, hearing the river flow, and watching the sun slowly rise, I never felt so zen in my life.

Getting to the airport was smooth, and I did not even dose off even though I was incredibly tired. Adrenaline was probably taking a control of me. 

The airport process also went very smooth, and I essentially stressed myself out for nothing. The flight desk attendant just swiped our passports, gave us our tickets, and sent us on our way with expedited security passes and lounge access. I was worried they were going to have to edit my ticket since I forgot to include my middle name on the booking and the last time I forgot to put my middle name on the booking was Hong Kong and the flight desk attendants got pretty mad. Luckily that was not the case and everything was seamless. 

Immigration was super smooth for me since I have three passports! I just scanned my Canadian passport and went on my way to the gate. Thank you, Mark Carney! I am so thankful I chose Japan to study abroad because I did not have to go through the visa process and have to obtain a reentry visa like Cole. He had to sit in the immigration line for over 30 minutes 🤣.

By the time Cole got out of immigration, we had to board. I have trouble understanding why people who are not even at the front of the plane in a rush to board so quickly, and even boarding a plane front to back is stupid. There has got to be a better way and people need to drop the "first come, first served" mentality and learn what "personal space" is. That was by far the biggest pet peeve I had 🤦‍♂️.
