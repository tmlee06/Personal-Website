The miso-curry-udon was a little less than $12. That would easily cost $25+ at Marugame Udon. Do not ever ask me if I want to go to Valley Fair again. I will never eat out in the States again unless it’s Little Ceasars or Costco 😂. Once I get home I know the depression is going to hit so hard, and Japan is going to be my whole personality. It’s incredibly difficult to adjust from cheap delicious food and goods, great public transit, walkable cities to overpriced gas, constant traffic, unsanitary cities, and denied insurance premiums. I paid for my health insurance and ice cream all for $30 at a convenience store 🤣. Luigi Mangione’s message is so good, but the way he “executed” it is intolerable. The goal in life is really to make an American salary and live in Japan 🤣. I know this sounds contradictory to what I said in the beginning, celebrating America, but America does have flaws like every country. One thing is for certain that although America is “not perfect, it’s not its mistakes.” Side note: Only One by Ye is a top 10 Ye song.

Hockey this weekend was frustrating. Dump-and-Chase hockey is so tiring. Every time my line-mates do that, they get off the ice immediately, which kills the momentum. My legs turn to jelly after the first period, I burn and use up all the caffeine from the pregame white-monster and electrolytes from the Aquarius in 30 minutes. I feel like a zombie when I’m out there at 1 AM. Everyone plays the game by the book here, which is good, but leaves no room for creativity. Hockey is fast-paced, changes on the fly, and you must be ready to adapt to the play. Teamwork and company work are mutually exclusive ideals in my opinion. I can not complain as much because I missed a lot of practices, and I am pretty much an independent contractor, but my style of play is always to benefit the team and defend my teammates or “co-workers” in this case. It’s funny how fun playing hockey in Japan was at first, then I realized how messed up the power dynamics of the senpai-kohai culture is, and how late practices/games are. I just feel bad for the lower class-men, 3rd, and 4th line that never get the chance to improve as much as the seniors. There was no reason why they should not have played this weekend. 4-2 W and 11-1 W (we were up by 5 in the first). The seniors are not that good to be doing all that. I did make one of the seniors bow to me 🤣. I ran out of cash to pay the senior for the curry-udon, so I offered to pay for the table with card. 3 udons for $36 what a steal. In the States, that’s one udon with tax and tip 💀. I am surprised I have not been billed by the team yet. The only thing I paid for was jerseys and my part of the BnB for Nagoya this weekend. Paying for the BnB was so pointless because our Friday night game ended at 3 am, we got to the BnB at 4, then had to check out at 10. Granted the drive from Kyoto to Nagoya is long, so I understand having to stay one night, but we spent the whole day up until our game at a super-sento, Japanese public bath lounge, which was a 24-hour one. I would’ve rather pay to stay longer at the sento than 6 hours at the BnB. Even though my Japanese is very limited, I enjoyed spending more time off-ice with the team. Team travel is always fun and I have been so lucky to have a great experience every time. No clue if you will ever see this, but I want to shout out the absolute goat of team managers, Simon Beckett. Simon you were always ready to adjust the itinerary to help me whether that be Pure Hockey runs because I broke my stick(s) or dropping me off at the airport because I had to fly home early to take an exam. You're the real MVP.
