My body cannot differentiate between night and day anymore, and my circadian rhythm is completely ruined. I could not sleep at all after posting last week's log. Tyler Durden has basically taken control of me, and I have fully become the Narrator during the day. On top of that, I have my AI and Japanese finals coming up. I am not even anxious about them, just genuinely exhausted from all the studying and non-stop grinding.

People are starting to pack up and clear out of the dorms, and honestly, it’s hitting me with this weird, unexpected sense of sadness. It’s strange because moving on isn't new to me at all. I’ve aged out of hockey teams, packed up at the end of summer camps, and moved out of UCSD dorms. I’ve done the whole saying goodbye to a space routine a million times. But watching people pack up their rooms here feels different. What trips me out the most is seeing people move out that I literally never even had a single conversation with. We shared the same hallways for months, lived a few doors apart, and now they are just gone.

It makes me genuinely fearful for the future and this whole loneliness epidemic everyone talks about. We live in a world obsessed with removing every bit of social friction by ordering everything on apps, avoiding awkward small talk, and keeping to ourselves. We are slowly optimizing our way into total isolation. Japan feels like the absolute paradise for that kind of risk-aversion culture, but the US is turning or is the exact same thing. Everyone, including myself, is constantly trying to hyper-optimize before feeling ready to actually do anything. We get caught in complete analysis paralysis. It brings me back to that scene in Good Will Hunting.

Having all the awareness in the world does not set you free if you use it as an excuse to freeze up. All it takes is a random twenty-second decision for something completely new to happen, but we or at least I talk ourselves out of taking the leap because we think we already know the ending. I fear of losing before I can think of winning. I probably have to talk to my shrink about that 😂.
